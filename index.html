What counts toward the score?

Draw the land borders shared by Portugal, Spain, France, Belgium, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, Germany, Switzerland, Austria, Italy, Denmark, the UK and Ireland. Borders with countries outside this region are excluded.

The counter updates after each stroke. A border is recognised when at least 60% of its length is within about 75 km of your drawing. This is a forgiving estimate of which borders you attempted; the score uses a stricter 25 km tolerance. Multiple strokes can cover one border; one stroke can cover several.

Coverage measures how much of the true border is within about 25 km of your lines. Accuracy measures how much of your drawing is within 25 km of a true border. Your score is their harmonic mean; both matter. Geography is simplified, and very small borders may be absent.

Made for curious minds. Not cartographers.
